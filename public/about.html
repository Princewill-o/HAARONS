THE BRAND STORY

The Evolution
of Presence.

H.AARONS begins with a question: what makes a presence memorable?

Presence is more than being seen. It is the atmosphere a person creates, the curiosity they spark and the impression they leave. Fragrance gives that feeling a form that can be experienced, even when it cannot immediately be explained.

The house is built around The Evolution of Presence: an exploration of how we are perceived, recognised and remembered. Its first principle is simple.

Every lasting presence
begins as a mystery.

That principle guides RHAPSODY, the first fragrance. The intention is to captivate without heaviness and invite a second encounter. Unexpected contrasts are part of the creative direction, with each element serving the experience of presence.

ABOUT THE FOUNDER

Henry Aaron
Opoku.

Henry is a London-based luxury fragrance consultant and the creator behind AaronScentials. His experience in luxury fragrance retail and his work sharing fragrances with an audience inform the direction of H.AARONS.

The motivation.

H.AARONS brings together his interest in fragrance, storytelling and unexpected contrasts. The ambition is to create a fragrance house with a clear point of view: compositions that make people curious, draw them closer and leave an impression.

RHAPSODY is the beginning of that ambition. Its development is guided by a question that runs through the house: does this deepen the experience of presence?

The vision sets the direction. Developing, testing and refining the fragrance is the work that gives it substance.

NO.01 RHAPSODY · THE STORY

One arrival.
An unanswered question.

A private dinner. Twelve guests. One empty seat.

A stranger enters.

By the end of the night, everyone remembers his presence, yet his story remains untold.

One question lingers:

Who is he?

Why this story?

RHAPSODY explores the first moment of perception: when someone captures our attention before we know who they are. The stranger is remembered through the atmosphere of his presence, while his identity remains a mystery.

The story is a creative scene, expressing the feeling the fragrance is intended to evoke. RHAPSODY is a free-flowing emergence of presence entering a space before explanation arrives.

The composition begins.

Its musical framing reflects that free-flowing beginning. RHAPSODY is the opening question of H.AARONS: a composition intended to unfold and reward closer attention.

The proposed scent direction brings bergamot and cardamom into a heart of cold iris, violet leaf and fig, followed by saffron, ambrette and sandalwood. Spice meets cool florals; softness meets texture. These notes remain a development brief as the fragrance is refined.
