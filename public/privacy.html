Launch list privacy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Who is responsible?

Henry Aaron Opoku, founder of H.AARONS, manages this launch list. For questions about your information or to request removal, contact the founder on Instagram.

What we collect and why

When you sign up, we store your email address, signup time, the version of the consent wording you accepted and a private removal token. We use this information to manage your subscription and send updates about RHAPSODY’s development and launch, with your permission.

Where it is stored

The list is stored in the website’s Cloudflare-hosted database. It is accessible to the site owner and the service providers needed to operate the website. We do not sell your email address. The website currently collects signups; it does not send automatic confirmation emails.

Leaving the list

Use the private removal link shown after signup, or contact the founder. Removing your subscription deletes your entry from the active list. Future launch emails will include a way to unsubscribe. We keep your entry while you are subscribed and this launch list remains active.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete your signup information, and withdraw your permission to receive updates. If you have a concern about how your information is handled, you can also contact the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.
