ABOUT H.AARONS

The person
behind the presence.

Founded by Henry Aaron Opoku, H.AARONS is an independent fragrance house built around The Evolution of Presence.

Silent Expression is our way of describing character communicated through fragrance. An impression that arrives before a word is spoken, and a memory that remains after the moment has passed. RHAPSODY marks the beginning.

When will RHAPSODY launch?

RHAPSODY is in development. A launch date has not yet been announced. Join the launch list for development updates and availability news.

The debut is being developed as a 100ml eau de parfum. The final composition and presentation will be confirmed as development progresses.

What will it cost?

Pricing has not yet been announced. Confirmed product details will be shared before release.

Does joining reserve a bottle?

No. The launch list is free to join and keeps you informed; it is not a purchase, preorder or bottle reservation. You can unsubscribe at any time.
